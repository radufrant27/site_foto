Hi, I'm Radu

A photographer who spends most weekends somewhere loud, and the weekdays editing what happened there.

Behind
the camera
The short version

Music first,
everything else
followed

I started photographing at shows, standing in front of stages long before anyone was paying me for it. That is still the work I love most — the two seconds when the light, the face and the sound line up and you either have it or you don't.

Portraits came next, because the same thing happens in a quiet room: you wait, you talk, and at some point the person stops performing. That is the frame I want.

And then weddings and christenings, which are really the same job again — one day, no second takes, a lot of people who deserve to be seen properly.

How it works

From first message
to final gallery

01

We talk

You send me the date, the place and roughly what you want. I answer with availability, price and any questions I need answered before I say yes.

02

We plan the small stuff

Schedule, access, stage times, the people who must be in the photos. For portraits: location, light, what to wear. Ten minutes now saves an hour later.

03

The shoot

I stay out of the way. Two bodies, fast primes, no flash unless the room really needs it. You will mostly forget I'm there, which is the point.

04

Selection and edit

I cull hard and edit every keeper by hand — colour, contrast, grain. Nothing goes out that I wouldn't put in my own portfolio.

05

Delivery

First previews within 48 hours, the full gallery as a private online album with full-resolution downloads. Yours to print, post and keep.

Gear, briefly

Tools, not
the point

Two full-frame bodies so nothing stops if one does, fast primes for dark venues, one zoom for when I can't move. Backed up twice before I leave the venue, every single time.

  • — 24mm / 35mm / 50mm / 85mm primes
  • — 70–200mm for stage distance
  • — Dual card slots, on-site backup

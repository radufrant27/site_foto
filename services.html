Questions

The ones
I get asked most

Do you travel?

Yes — anywhere in Romania and across Europe. Travel inside my home city is included; beyond that I add fuel or a ticket at cost, agreed with you before booking.

How do I reserve a date?

A signed agreement and a deposit hold the date. The rest is due on the day of the shoot or on delivery, whichever we agree.

How fast do I get the photos?

Previews within 48 hours for everything. Full galleries: 5 days for portraits, 72 hours for festivals, 3–4 weeks for weddings.

Do you deliver RAW files?

No. The edit is part of the work you're hiring me for. If you need a specific frame retouched differently, tell me and I'll do it.

What if it rains, or the venue is very dark?

Both are normal. Rain makes better photographs than a flat grey sky, and dark venues are where I spend most of my time — that's what fast lenses are for.

Can we add hours on the day?

Usually yes, if I don't have another booking after. Extra hours are charged at the rate we agreed up front, never improvised.
